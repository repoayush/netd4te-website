Privacy Policy

Last Updated: July 2026 | Status: In Production
Notice: This Privacy Policy is currently in production. It will be finalized before the official NETD4TE app launch. The rules below show our promise to protect your privacy.

1. Introduction

NETD4TE Inc. ("we," "us," or "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our website or mobile app.

We believe privacy is not a feature. It is a foundation. As a dating platform, we understand how sensitive your information is. We take that responsibility very seriously.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Personal Information

When you sign up for early access or create an account, we may collect:

  • Name and email address
  • Date of birth and age
  • Profile photos and bio
  • Location (only if you allow it)
  • Gender identity and dating preferences

2.2 Usage Data

We collect information about how you use our platform:

  • Device type and operating system
  • IP address and log data
  • How you use features in the app
  • Match and conversation data

2.3 AI and Analytics Data

Our AI Digital Wingman may look at conversation patterns to improve matching. This data is anonymized and used only to make the platform better.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide and improve our dating services
  • Help you find meaningful matches
  • Keep users safe and verify who they are
  • Send updates about new features
  • Follow legal rules and prevent fraud
  • Train and improve our AI matching system

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We only share information in these cases:

  • Service Providers: Trusted companies that help us run the platform (hosting, analytics, support)
  • Legal Requirements: When required by law or to protect our rights
  • Business Changes: If we merge, sell, or transfer our business
  • With Your Permission: When you explicitly say we can share it

5. Data Security

We use industry-standard security including:

  • End-to-end encryption for sensitive messages
  • Secure socket layer (SSL) technology
  • Regular security checks and testing
  • Strict access controls for our team
  • Optional two-factor authentication

6. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to:

  • Access, correct, or delete your data
  • Object to or limit how we use your data
  • Download your data in a portable format
  • Withdraw your consent at any time
  • Opt out of marketing emails

To use these rights, email us at privacy@netd4te.com.

7. Cookies

We use cookies and similar tools to improve your experience, analyze traffic, and personalize content. You can manage cookies in your browser settings.

8. Children's Privacy

NETD4TE is only for users aged 18 and older. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If we find out a minor gave us personal information, we will delete it immediately.

9. International Data

Your information may be stored and processed in countries other than your own. We make sure proper protections are in place to keep your data safe.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of big changes by email or in the app. If you keep using our services after changes, you accept the updated policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact our Privacy Office:

Email: privacy@netd4te.com
Security: security@netd4te.com

Production Note: This Privacy Policy is being reviewed by our legal team and outside privacy experts. The final version will be published before the official NETD4TE app launch. It will follow GDPR, CCPA, and all other data protection laws.
